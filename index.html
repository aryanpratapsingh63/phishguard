💡 Cybersecurity Awareness Note: Anatomy of Phishing URLs

Phishing attacks rely heavily on psychological deception and deceptive URL formatting to mislead victims before they realize their credentials have been harvested. Common deceptive tactics include:

  • Subdomain Brand Hijacking: Placing trusted names (e.g. paypal.com.attacker-controlled.net) in the subdomain while the apex domain belongs to the adversary.
  • Direct IP Hosts: Avoiding domain registration records and WHOIS lookups by directing targets to bare IPv4/IPv6 addresses.
  • Credential Deception (RFC 3986): Embedding target domain names before an @ symbol to distract from the true destination host.
  • Homograph & Punycode Mimicry: Replacing ASCII letters with visually identical Cyrillic characters (e.g. xn--gogle-qoa.com).
